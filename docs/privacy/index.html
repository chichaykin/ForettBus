Privacy Policy for Forett Shuttle

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Forett Shuttle provides the Forett Condo shuttle timetable, optional shuttle reminders, public-bus arrival estimates, and optional trip-planning tools. This policy explains how the Android application handles information.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your selected direction and appearance preference, any timetable changes you approve, saved trip cards (such as place labels, addresses, bus-stop codes, routes, and service numbers), and an active reminder. This information is stored in the app's local storage on your device. The app does not create an account or operate a user database.

You can remove this information by deleting individual trip cards, restoring the original timetable, cancelling a reminder, or clearing the app's storage/uninstalling the app in Android settings.

Photos, files, and camera

If you choose Import photo, the app asks you to take a photo or choose one from your device. The image is used on the device to recognise timetable text for your review. If you choose Import CSV, the selected file is read on the device for the same purpose. Photos and CSV files are not uploaded by the app, and imported timetable changes are saved only after you confirm them.

Notifications

If you enable a shuttle reminder, the app requests Android notification permission and, where required, permission to schedule an exact alarm. The reminder is scheduled locally on your device. The app does not send notification content or device tokens to a server.

Network requests and transport information

To show live public-bus estimates, the app sends the selected bus-stop code and service selection to the Forett Shuttle service hosted on Cloudflare. That service obtains transit-arrival information from Singapore's Land Transport Authority (LTA) DataMall. Responses may be temporarily cached to provide the service.

If you use place search or Find alternatives, the app sends the place search text, addresses or coordinates you select, and requested journey date/time to the Forett Shuttle service, which requests matching results from OneMap. The app also requests Singapore public-holiday data from data.gov.sg to determine the shuttle operating calendar. These features are optional; you can use the bundled timetable without them.

Network providers necessarily receive technical request information, such as your IP address, browser/app request headers, and the requested URL, and may process it under their own policies. The app uses Cloudflare, LTA DataMall, OneMap, and data.gov.sg only to provide the transport features described above. Their handling is governed by their respective privacy notices, including Cloudflare's Privacy Policy, LTA's Privacy Statement, OneMap's Privacy Statement, and data.gov.sg's Privacy Statement.

What we do not do

Forett Shuttle does not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs, does not sell personal information, does not use precise device location, and does not collect contacts, microphone recordings, payment information, or identifiers for advertising.

Children

The app is a timetable and transport-planning utility and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The latest version will be published at this address and the date above will be updated.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Forett Management Office at mo@forettcondo.sg.
