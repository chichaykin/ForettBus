Install Forett Shuttle on iPhone

  1. Open the Forett Shuttle link in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Forett Shuttle from its new Home Screen icon.
  4. When you enable reminders, allow notifications.

The timetable remains available without installing. iPhone reminders require the Home Screen app and iOS 16.4 or later.
