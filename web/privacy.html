Forett Shuttle Privacy Policy

Forett Shuttle is an independent resident app and is not operated by the Management Office.

Stored on your device

Your theme, direction, imported timetable and route cards stay in this browser. They are not synchronized to other devices. Clearing website data removes them.

Online requests

The app sends the stop, route or place-search details needed to obtain public transport information. It does not require a name, email address or resident account.

Reminders

If you enable notifications, the server stores a random installation session, the browser push subscription, departure time, direction and timetable revision. Finished reminder jobs are retained for no more than seven days. Invalid subscriptions are deleted when detected. Disabling notifications removes the subscription and cancels pending jobs.
