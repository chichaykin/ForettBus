Forett Shuttle Privacy Policy

Forett Shuttle is an independent resident app and is not operated by the Management Office.

Stored on your device

Your theme, direction, imported timetable and route cards stay in this browser. They are not synchronized to other devices. Use Back up app data in Profile to save a copy before changing devices or clearing website data. The backup file contains these settings and routes, not notification permissions or push credentials.

Online requests

The app does not require a name, email address or resident account. It creates a random installation session so the service can limit requests fairly. The app sends stop, route, place-search and trip-planning details needed to obtain public transport information. These requests may include stop codes or selected places and coordinates.

Usage analytics

Usage analytics for the web app is on by default. Google Analytics 4 receives page and screen views and basic actions such as setting a reminder or saving a route card. It uses a browser identifier and first-party analytics cookies to distinguish visits. The app does not send your card names, routes, addresses, bus stops, timetable, email address or phone number as analytics events. Events recorded while offline may be kept in this browser for up to 72 hours and sent when it reconnects. Events older than 72 hours are discarded.

Turn off Usage analytics in Profile at any time. This stops future analytics requests, clears pending offline events and removes analytics cookies from this installation. It does not erase events already included in Google Analytics reports. The preference stays on this installation and is not changed by restoring an app-data backup.

Reminders

If you enable notifications, the server stores the random installation session, browser push subscription, departure time, direction and timetable revision. Installation sessions expire after 180 days without renewal. Finished reminder jobs are retained for no more than seven days. Invalid push subscriptions are deleted when detected. Disabling notifications removes the subscription and cancels pending jobs. Notification display time depends on network and device settings.
